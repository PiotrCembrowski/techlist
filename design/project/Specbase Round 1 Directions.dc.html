Three directions

All three sit inside the Industry system — Barlow family, one steel accent, square hairline frames — and differ in structure, density and voice. Each board shows identity, the homepage top, a product card (light and dark) and the top of a product page. Every brand, product and retailer shown is fictional.

1a

Drafting Sheet

Industry at full strength: every product, plate and offer is drawn as a hairline object with registration marks, on a cool grey ground with steel as the single accent. Condensed headings fit long labels into narrow cells, so dense spec grids stay calm. It reads as an engineering reference — exact, a little austere.

Notes common to all three Accent text and primary fills step to the system's 700 (#416180) in light mode; the 500 base misses 4.5:1 on the grey ground. Dark mode uses the 400 step (#94bce3). Semantic colours (success, stale, danger) are muted additions tuned to the same lightness as the steel ramp; each status also carries an icon and a word. All fonts are on Google Fonts (OFL); numerals are tabular everywhere. Icons are Lucide at 1.5 stroke.
